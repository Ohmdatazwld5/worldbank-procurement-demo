How to read this demo

The app shows retrieved passages with their source IDs, project-record claims, and the risk method. A “GROUNDED” status means the response passed the app’s citation checks; it does not mean the underlying data is real or independently verified.

For real project analysis, replace the sample feed and fictional documents with reviewed, authoritative records. The AI Procurement Contracts workbook is a separate dataset for source/year counts; it is not connected to this Q&A endpoint.
